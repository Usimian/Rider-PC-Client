The sensor's 8 × 8 view, looking ahead: left = the robot's left, top row = farthest. Colour = distance (red near, blue far; numbers in cm). Outline: obstacle, floor, floor dropped away. The bar marks the drive path (the columns the stop uses).

Each sensor zone as a point in the room, corrected for pitch, roll and leg height: the same points the obstacle stop decides from. Obstacle (with a stalk to the floor), floor, floor dropped away, grey = overhead. Grid = 10 cm on the floor; the shaded lane is the drive path: amber = slowing, red = stopping. Drag sideways to turn, up/down to tilt; middle-button drag moves it; scroll to zoom. Phone: one finger turns/tilts, two fingers pinch to zoom and move. Reset view (or a mouse double-click) goes back to the start.
